# Reflect

Review a completed task and identify lessons that should become durable structure. Do not modify skills automatically.

## Procedure

1. Read the relevant transcript, plan, diff, verification output, and handoff.
2. Identify repeated friction, repeated corrections, missing guardrails, and successful decisions.
3. Separate a one-off task detail from a reusable workflow lesson.
4. Map each lesson to the single skill that should own it.
5. Propose a focused edit, lint, metadata field, script, or verification check.
6. Ask for approval before changing the owning skill.

## Verification

Every proposed lesson has evidence from the completed work. No lesson creates a duplicate source of truth. The proposed owner and concrete structural change are explicit.
